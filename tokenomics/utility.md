# MATCH Utility

MATCH is designed to support the MATCHKR arena ecosystem.

### Primary Utilities

1. **Match Entry Fee**
   - Small entry fee in MATCH to join competitive matches (partially burned or added to reward pool).

2. **Staking for Ranking Boost**
   - Stake MATCH to receive ranking weight or priority in matchmaking.

3. **Spectator Features**
   - Access advanced spectator tools and historical analytics.

4. **Fee Sharing & Buybacks**
   - A portion of arena-related volume and future features can be used for MATCH buybacks and rewards.

5. **Governance (Future)**
   - Token holders may vote on arena parameters (rules, seasons, rewards).

### Design Philosophy

Utility is built gradually based on real usage of the arena.  
The token primarily benefits from trading activity and fee generation through the Bankr launch mechanism.
